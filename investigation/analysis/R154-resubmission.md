# R154: bounded queued-preemption resubmission contract

Source-derived KMD correction, implemented offline on 2026-09-28. No package, Air access, firmware change, or hardware run. EXP867's branch7/Flags0x80 proves a deterministic envelope rejection, but its DMA VA differs from stalled fence36357: fixing it is independently justified and does not establish the TDR cause.

Sources inspected: `gpuva_g3_windows.c` (submit, private find/reap/cancel/release/retire, BeginJob), `scheduler_windows.c` (queued preempt/reset), `submission_windows.c` (CancelCommand), `callbacks.c` (DestroyContext), shared `apple_agx_scheduler.c`, Mesa `agx_win32_gpuva_batch.c` and `agx_win32_gpuva.c`, `g4_submit_virtual_replay.c`, `g3_r137_private_combined.c`, and pinned WDK26100 `d3dkmddi.h:4305`. The worktree `.local/tandem/REVIEW.md` was absent. Hardware/Asahi/m1n1/Mu protocols are not changed by this host ownership contract; no new source claim about those layers is required.

Microsoft documents Resubmission as a normal WDDM2+ packet flag following preemption: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ddi/d3dkmddi/ns-d3dkmddi-_dxgk_submitcommandflags . The pinned header identifies bit7 (0x80). The official preemption contract assigns NEW fence IDs to nonpaging packets: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/display/gpu-preemption . Preserve the scheduler's monotonic fence check; same-fence render resubmission is not required. Other flags retain their current rejection semantics.

The original violation had two layers of one lifecycle: the envelope requires Flags==0; queued preemption then publishes generic PrivateCancel, while FindPrivateScene rejects the still-Queued scene. Adding generic reap before lookup is insufficient: reap may release a ReleaseRequested scene, and lookup independently rejects ReleaseRequested. Mesa normally requests release only after WaitRender retirement (or rejection), not immediately after submission; nevertheless the existing KMD explicitly accepts deferred release while Queued, so that accepted lifetime must remain safe during preemption.

Implemented ownership contract:

1. Admit only zero or WDK Resubmission flags (prefer a typed flags copy with Resubmission cleared, then require Value==0). Keep every envelope/parser/graph/geometry/output/owner validation. Do not interpret the flag as GPU completion or authorize replay of Started work.
2. Distinguish certain queued preemption from final cancellation. Add a separate exact preempt-fence marker on the context and a DPC-safe publisher used ONLY after scheduler queued discard and backend-image release succeeded. Existing cancellation/reset-uncertain paths keep their semantics. Keep the scene's Queued hold and old Fence while suspended; generic reaping must not free it. No allocations, mapping, graph operations, or fast mutex acquisition at DPC.
3. Extend private lookup with explicit resubmission admission: an exact same context/manager-generation/scene-generation lease may reuse its suspended hold only when !Submitting, !Started, !Quarantined, no uncertain/final-cancel marker, and old scene Fence == context PrivateFence == preempt marker. Permit ReleaseRequested for that existing suspended transaction only. Ordinary submits still reject queued/released scenes; an invented resubmission with no matching suspended transaction must fail for v3. Revalidate all packet accesses and geometry before changing ownership.
4. After successful validation, transfer that retained hold to the NEW SubmissionFenceId, clear the exact preempt marker, and queue through the existing scheduler. No second scene allocation, no content-based identity exceptions, and no monotonic fence relaxation. BeginJob must reject a still-suspended hold. On bind/prepare/queue rollback restore the prior suspended hold/old fence/marker under the state lock (or retain a terminal hold for context teardown); never free it opportunistically or create a GPU completion. Preserve Submitting protection throughout local-pointer rollback.
5. Teardown is the final owner when no resubmission arrives. DestroyContext already requires Object.FenceOutstanding==0 and calls PrivateRetireContext at PASSIVE. Once GpuvaG3Closing is set, an exact suspended, never-started, certain hold may be discharged under the state lock and released through the normal private release path. Started/uncertain/quarantined holds remain pinned. Reset/final cancellation must supersede a suspended marker without converting uncertainty into reclaim permission. A release escape before resubmission keeps the suspended hold until actual completion or context teardown.

Ownership: Windows supplies resubmission and new fence; KMD scheduler discards queue/image and publishes exact suspension at DPC; KMD private lifecycle pins context/storage and performs PASSIVE transfer/reclaim; GPU submission remains BeginJob/backend-owned. UMD release relinquishes its future use, not the driver's outstanding suspended transaction. No m1n1/Mu/Asahi, DMA mapping, power, IRQ, signer, or caps modification follows from this defect.

Regression contract: (a) valid flags0x80 succeeds and every other single bit/mixed unsupported bit fails; (b) real queued submit→real queued preempt→new-fence resubmit→BeginJob→joined completion/report works, old fence never completes; (c) same lifecycle with ReleaseRequested before and after preemption retains exact scene backing and frees only after report; (d) generic reap between preempt/resubmit preserves hold; (e) wrong context/generation, duplicate/reused fence, no preempt marker, Started, uncertain, stale cancel, and malformed resubmit reject without losing hold; (f) bind/queue failure preserves owner/teardown safety; (g) closing a suspended never-started context frees once while uncertain context stays busy. The original G4 replay stubbed PrivateReap and scheduler. The implemented coverage below links the real scheduler and uses complementary real private-lifecycle replay; a flags-only test is insufficient.

Verdict: the complete KMD ownership correction passes offline component replays. A mask-only change or generic reap-before-find is incomplete. Hardware checkpoint, if separately authorized: exact queued-preempt receipt followed by same lease/new-fence admitted and one natural completion, with unchanged package/recovery contracts. Until then status is implemented only, never hardware-validated, and no claim that EXP867's active36357 TDR is fixed.
