**Source inspected:** EXP906 original UMD/per-process KMD query receipt, TDR dump header/cdb and recovery manifest; `investigation/analysis/PRESENT-PATH-AUDIT.md`; pinned WDK26100 GPUVA Present, MakeResident, callback, scheduler and TDR DDIs; current native Asahi `agx_flush_batch` generated from `drivers/apple-agx/mesa/scripts/native-asahi-batch-lifecycle.py`, `agx_win32_gpuva.c`, UMD `umd_gpuva_windows.c`/`umd.c`, KMD `gpuva_g3_windows.c`, `backend_platform_windows.c`, `paging_windows.c`, `present_windows.c`, `display.c`/`scanout_windows.c`, m1n1 retained GPUVA/DCP code, and Mu R143 AGX ACPI. Microsoft documents explicit residency and paging-fence waits, the DXGI Present context/handle callback, KMD Present/virtual submit, TDR reset, AutoLogger `LogFileMode`/`MaxFileSize`, CrashControl and WER mini/count settings. See the primary links in `PRESENT-PATH-AUDIT.md` and [AutoLogger](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/etw/configuring-and-starting-an-autologger-session), [WER LocalDumps](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/wer/collecting-user-mode-dumps), [CrashControl](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/windows-server/performance/memory-dump-file-options).

**WINDOWS CONTRACT:** VidMM's 4 KiB/64 KiB GPUVA residency is authoritative for CPU staging copies. `MakeResident` E_PENDING requires the paging fence before referenced commands; a Present callback carries the created context, source allocation and unchanged opaque DXGI context. Dxgkrnl may queue DMA and Present behind context fences, and TDR names the outstanding packet/reset failure. A KMD BLT must complete or legally fault its fence before the primary can be displayed.

**AGX/ASAHI CONTRACT:** Native Asahi VDM/clear work is flushed before Present. The KMD translation maps VidMM resident 4 KiB PTEs into local backing, while m1n1 manages AGX UAT and DCP surface/swap. Mu exposes APPL0002/local reserve/synthetic IRQ. DCP latches a specific IOVA and local pool PA; Windows primary BLT writes must resolve to that same backing.

**TRANSLATION:** The sole independently observable driver behavior change is R185 commit `f096b05687f8955bf391a1fa77ec6cf1d94d8cd2`: G3 COPY_QUERY/UPLOAD/DOWNLOAD validates each 4 KiB `ResidentPtes` owner/offset/segment/range under lock without first requiring an AGX 16 KiB published leaf. Actual extracted KMD replay historical RED predicate53 then GREEN accepts a fully resident scattered range and rejects invalid provenance. R186 commit `ddd8498b44b00161ca9b7ab8405390edf2e9f7f5` is diagnostic-only: a DWM UMD arm escape identifies up to eight same-context/VA entries; UMD logs render and Present fences; KMD records per-context query/submit/completion/Present/BLT destination; TDR snapshots outstanding packet/context/fence, last completed fence and reset status. Read-only one-shot probe and early periodic polling expose the state. The m1n1 EXP890 late-scanout firmware/Mu R143 remain byte-identical. Dump/trace settings change diagnostics only: one kernel dump with overwrite, WER mini/count1 plus 128 MiB launch/window folder budget, circular ETL max256 MiB. Windows WER has no native per-file byte limit; the budget is an operational gate, not a filesystem quota.

**WHAT IS STILL UNKNOWN:** EXP906 proves DWM PID1244/handlec0000a80/VA7d0000 hit predicate53 but did not capture its resident PTE state. R185 may or may not admit that exact allocation. EXP905's callback S_OK versus KMD Present0 lacks a same-context queued packet/fence relation. EXP906's `0x116` at +82.918 s has an AppleAgx TDR bucket but no initiating context/fence or private reset predicate. A live DWM BLT's destination IPA has not been matched to the DCP latched PA. The three bounded receipts are designed to distinguish these without changing the driver outcome.

**WHY THIS HYPOTHESIS:** (1) R185 replay shows a real deterministic graph-versus-resident representation mismatch and EXP906 names a live DWM predicate53 at the same KMD guard. (2) EXP905 proves DWM callback S_OK yet KMD Present0 in four probes while unrelated non-Present virtual submits continue, so a context/fence-linked scheduler receipt is needed. (3) EXP906's `0x116` at +82.918 s prevented all late snapshots and the planned counter probe; the new early polling and TDR snapshot can identify the exact outstanding packet even on another early reset. These facts favor one R185 behavior package with passive receipts over an unchanged rerun or speculative DCP/KMD policy patch.
